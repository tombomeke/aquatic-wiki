# Every Abyss Fruit and its moves


Use a move with **sneak + F** (or sneak + right-click a block); **sneak + scroll** with an empty hand picks the move. Every fruit has two or three moves and an **awakening move** that unlocks at **mastery 25** (mastery goes to 40). Damage is the base number: fruit moves deal ×2.6, times the rarity power and +2.0% per mastery level.

The sea weakens every fruit user (sinking, slowness, no powers in water), except **Tidecaller**. Rain does not.

| Rarity | Fruits |
| --- | --- |
| Common (power ×0.9) | Dunemaw, Venomfang, Springcoil, Stoneskin |
| Uncommon (power ×1.0) | Behemoth, Galeborn, Anchorweight, Ghostglass |
| Rare (power ×1.15) | Packfang, Lodestone, Rigline, Titanseed |
| Epic (power ×1.35) | Mirrorscale, Riftshift, Sirensong, Emberheart, Luminara, Rimefrost |
| Legendary (power ×1.6) | Krakenheart, Chronoshard, Hollowvoid, Thunderveil, Tidecaller |
