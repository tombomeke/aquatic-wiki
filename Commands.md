# Commands

Every command a player can use, by topic. `<this>` is something you fill in, `[this]` is optional.
In game, `/aquatic commands` lists them all (click one for its help page), and every command shows its own
help when you type it without anything after it.

Most things also have a menu or an NPC: the shopkeepers for the market, the Crew Registrar and `/crew` for
your crew, `/island manage` for your island. You rarely need to type the long commands.
