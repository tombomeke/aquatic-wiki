# Treasure and ores

## Buried treasure
- Treasure lies buried on islands, marked by a **red X** of wool. Dig **straight down** under the middle of the X.
- Depth: Common 1 block, Uncommon and Rare 2, Epic 3, Legendary 4. First to open the chest gets the Doubloons and the loot, on any island.
- **Treasure maps** show a red X *near* the treasure. Right-click the map: it says if you're getting warmer or colder.
- `/treasure info` gives rumours about what's buried and where.
- **MEGA legendary treasure** is rare: a beam of light, fireworks and a pile of loot.

## Tidewatch forts and ships at sea
Tidewatch forts are guarded by the navy: kill the captain to liberate the island (everyone who helped gets paid, and
the fort's strongbox is buried in the keep). Pirate and Tidewatch ships sail the open sea on their own: board one,
beat the crew, loot the chests and get off before it sinks. Your own ship: see [Ships](Ships.md).

## Which island has which ores?
Resources are **scarce** on purpose: protect what you have, build farms, trade. Every island has a little **coal, iron and copper** and about **100 diamonds** (deep down, where the caves lead). On top of that, every island type has its own signature. Numbers for an island of average size (radius ~110), the average of two freshly generated islands per type:

| Island | Signature | Coal | Iron | Copper | Gold | Redstone | Lapis | Diamonds | Emeralds | Quartz |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Volcanic** | **diamonds**, quartz, obsidian | 880 | 910 | 0 | 310 | 0 | 0 | **430** | 0 | **280** |
| **Jungle** | **emeralds** | 1,820 | 1,380 | 30 | 450 | 20 | 0 | 110 | **150** | 0 |
| **Ancient** | **redstone**, a few emeralds | 870 | 870 | 40 | 10 | **830** | 10 | 110 | 45 | 0 |
| **Desert** | **gold** | 480 | 480 | 360 | **420** | 10 | 0 | 110 | 0 | 0 |
| **Snowy** | **lapis** | 1,160 | 850 | 570 | 10 | 20 | **560** | 110 | 0 | 0 |
| **Tropical** | **copper** | 850 | 600 | **1,680** | 10 | 40 | 10 | 90 | 0 | 0 |
| **Forest** | **coal** | **1,640** | 880 | 820 | 20 | 10 | 20 | 100 | 0 | 0 |
| **Tidewatch fort** | **iron** | 620 | **1,270** | 450 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 60 | 0 | 0 |
| Plains | only the basics: the farming island | 980 | 640 | 850 | 20 | 0 | 10 | 90 | 0 | 0 |
| Village | only the basics: villages trade | 670 | 490 | 490 | 10 | 0 | 20 | 70 | 0 | 0 |

The numbers grow with the size of the island. The normal Minecraft ores in the sea floor under an island are removed when it's generated, so digging down under your island doesn't give you extra diamonds. These numbers are for islands generated from version 2.38.0 on; older islands keep what they had.

## Volcanic islands
At night, **blazes and magma cubes** spawn on volcanic islands instead of zombies and skeletons. Bring fire resistance.

## Villages
Villages are neutral: you can't build, break or fight there, and **real villagers** live and walk around in them. You can't hurt them.
