# What is Aquatic SMP?

Aquatic SMP is a pirate survival server. There is no continent: the world is one big ocean, the **Endless Sea**,
with hundreds of generated islands on it. Everything you do starts with a boat.

## The idea in one line

**Sail. Claim. Conquer.** You sail out from the capital, your crew claims islands, the islands make goods, you carry
those goods home across a sea full of other pirates, and other crews try to take your islands from you.

## How a pirate's life goes

**Your first hour.** You spawn in the capital, a harbour town with a market, shopkeepers and piers. A camera tour shows
you around: where to found a crew, where to buy a ship, where to buy ship parts if you'd rather build your own. You start
with 2,000 Doubloons, enough for a crew and a small raft. For your first 30 minutes nobody can attack you.

**Finding your island.** Islands are generated with their own terrain, caves, ores and ruins. Plains and forests are
common near the capital; jungles, deserts and snowy islands a bit farther out; tropical, volcanic and ancient islands
are rare and far away. Most of them are wild: something already lives there (bandits, squatters, temple guardians),
and claiming one means beating them first.

**Making it pay.** A claimed island produces the trade good of its type, also while you are offline. Pack it into
cargo crates and sail them to a Harbor Exchange. Crates are heavy: you can't teleport with them, and if you die or
your boat is sunk, they go overboard for anyone to take. The farther you carry them, the more they fetch. That trip
is where most of the fights happen.

**Keeping it.** Every crew has a daily siege window in the evening. In it, another crew can declare a siege on one of
your islands and try to hold the ground around your flag. Your facilities fight back: a Watchtower shows the
attackers, a Shore Battery shoots at their ships, Barracks send guards. Every facility they break speeds up the
capture, so you can't just stand on the flag; you have to spread out and defend your whole island.

**Getting stronger.** Abyss Fruits (23 of them, from Common to Legendary) give you powers, at the price of never
swimming again. Fruits level up with use and awaken at mastery 25. Pirates without a fruit train their **Tidewill**,
which makes fruit powers hurt them less. Custom weapons (cutlasses, flintlocks, muskets, hand cannons) and Abyssal
Iron, which silences fruit powers, make sure a fruit is strong but never everything.

**Everything else.** Buried treasure under a red X and treasure maps for the race to it, Tidewatch forts to
liberate, pirate ships at sea to board and sink, village quests, bounties on the most wanted heads, crates and a
gambling den, server events in the [Coliseum](Coliseum.md), and the Pirate King tournament, whose winner sits on the
throne in the capital as the **Pirate King**.

## What makes it different

- **The sea matters.** Most servers make travel something you skip. Here the trip is the game: cargo can't
  teleport, the far sea pays the most and is the most dangerous, and real ships (built block by block, with engines
  and cannons) fight on the water.
- **Claiming is conquering.** Islands aren't free land you protect with a command. You take them from what lives
  there, and other crews can take them from you, but only in a fair window you chose, never at 4 am.
- **Fruits without the grind wall.** Powers are strong and fun, but the sea, Abyssal Iron and Tidewill keep
  fruitless pirates in the game.
