# Combat

## PvP rules
- **Combat tag:** hitting or being hit by a player puts you both *in combat* for 15 seconds (red bar at the top). No `/crew home`, `/spawn`, `/tpa` or other escapes.
- **Logging out in combat kills you:** your items drop and your attacker gets the kill and the bounty.
- **Respawn protection:** 10 seconds after respawning (attacking ends it).
- **New-player protection:** your first 30 minutes. `/pvp` shows your status.

## Bounties
Beating pirates raises your bounty and earns titles, from Rookie to Emperor. Kill a wanted pirate to claim their bounty. `/bounty info`, `/bounty list`, `/bounty set <player> <amount>`, `/wanted` (a poster).

## Pirate levels
XP comes from PvP kills, monsters, treasure, discovering islands and bounties. Every level gives a little extra damage, every 5 levels an extra heart, plus rewards and a title. `/level`, `/level top`.

## Gear
Every custom weapon, gun, armour set, drink and bomb, with what it does and where to get it, is on [Items](Items.md).
**Abyssal Iron** weapons switch off a fruit user's power for a few seconds. **The Smithy** (`/smithy`, or the
Blacksmith at the capital) upgrades the item in your hand from +1 to +5; a failed attempt costs the materials but never
breaks the item.

## Tidewill
The will of the sea, a strength **every pirate** can train, with or without a fruit. Every hit you take from a fruit power trains it, and each level makes fruit powers hurt you less. `/tidewill` shows your **Will Rank** (1 to 50). Train it faster by sparring with a friend: `/spar <player>` (nobody dies). More is coming: Ironwill, Seasight and Stormcrown.
