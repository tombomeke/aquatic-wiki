# Questions and words

## "Why can't I ...?"

**...teleport?** You're carrying cargo crates (you're *laden*: sail them home), you're in a fight (wait for the
combat tag to run out, `/pvp` shows it), or you're trying to go to an island under siege (sail there).
[Cargo and trade](Cargo-and-Trade.md)

**...build on this island?** It belongs to another crew. Only the owners and their allies build there, and only the
besieging crew during a siege. On your own island, your crew may have limited building to officers
(`/island manage`). [Islands](Islands.md)

**...claim this island?** You need a crew, you must be its Captain or First Mate, the crew bank needs the price, and a
crew owns at most 3 islands. The capital and villages can't be claimed, and a Tidewatch fort must be liberated first.

**...hurt that player?** One of you is still in new-player protection (your first 30 minutes; `/pvp`), you're in
Harbour Waters around the capital (no fighting there), or you're crewmates or allies.

**...swim?** You ate an Abyss Fruit: the sea takes your power and you sink. You get a few seconds to get out of the
water. Keep a boat close. [Abyss Fruits](Abyss-Fruits.md)

**...declare a siege?** It's not the owners' siege window (`/island info` shows it, in server time), the island had a
siege in the last 24 hours, your crew already declared two today, or you're more than 300 blocks away. [Sieges](Sieges.md)

**...disband my crew?** Not during a siege your crew is in. Wait until it's over.

**...watch the intro again?** Only at spawn: `/spawn`, then `/guide intro`.

**...pay 1,5 Doubloons?** Doubloons are whole numbers. `1.500`, `1,500` and `1.5k` all mean fifteen hundred.

## "Where do I ...?"

| I want to... | Go to |
| --- | --- |
| Buy tools, food, gear, building blocks | The shopkeepers in the market stalls at the capital |
| Sell loot | The Merchant at the capital buys loot and monster parts; the Provisioner buys fish and farm produce |
| Get a netherite upgrade template | The Merchant at the capital sells them |
| Found a crew | The Crew Registrar at the capital, or `/crew` |
| Buy a ship | The Shipbuilder at the end of a pier |
| Buy an engine or ship cannons | The Shipwright (Sailing stall) |
| Sell cargo crates | A Harbor Exchange (the capital and villages) |
| Upgrade a sword | The Blacksmith at the capital (the Smithy) |
| Join a fight | The [Coliseum](Coliseum.md): step into the end portal at spawn, then `/seaevent join` when an event starts |
| Find quests | The Harbourmaster at the capital, and the villagers by every village well |
| Gamble | The Dealer in the capital's gambling den |
| Find an island | `/island map`, or a Tide Compass (right-click: a random island, sneak + right-click: the nearest) |

## Words you'll see

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Doubloons** | The money. `/doubloons` shows yours |
| **Crew bank / treasury** | Your crew's shared money: upgrades, facilities and sieges are paid from it |
| **Wild island** | An island something already lives on: claiming it means beating them first |
| **Tamed** | An island that recently lost its owner: claimable without a fight for 6 hours |
| **Facility** | A building your crew puts on its island (Dock, Watchtower, Shore Battery...) |
| **Upgrade** | A level bought for an island (Treasury, Fortress, Barracks...) |
| **Siege window** | The daily time slot (server time) when your islands can be attacked |
| **Core** | The glowing block on a facility during a siege: break it to speed up the capture |
| **Laden** | Carrying cargo crates: no teleporting, slower, and the crates drop if you die |
| **Harbour Waters** | The safe sea around the capital: no fighting |
| **The Reach** | The Abyssal Reach, the far sea: the richest goods, squalls |
| **Abyss Fruit** | A rare fruit that gives a power and takes your swimming |
| **Mastery** | How trained your fruit is (up to 40); at 25 it awakens |
| **Tidewill / Will Rank** | Resistance to fruit powers, trained by taking fruit hits and sparring |
| **Bounty** | The price on your head. Beat a wanted pirate to claim theirs |
| **Tidewatch** | The Harbor Crown's navy: forts, sloops and soldiers |
| **Pirate King** | The winner of the Pirate King tournament, who wears the crown and sits on the throne |
| **The Coliseum** | The arena islands where the fight events and tournaments are held: the end portal at spawn takes you there |
