# Ships

You start with a plain boat. A real ship is built from blocks, has an engine, seats for your crew and room for
cannons, and it can be sunk.

## Buy one

The **Shipbuilder** stands at the end of every pier on the capital and in the villages, and on every crew's Dock
facility (only that crew and its allies can buy there). Right-click him, pick a ship and click twice to buy.

The ship is built in the water next to the pier and you get its **engine**. Place the engine on the deck where the
green sparkles are within 20 minutes, sit on the stair at the back and sail away. Until it leaves the pier, only
your crew can build on it or break it. One purchase every 30 minutes; ships are never bought back.

## Or build your own

Cheaper, and any shape you like:

1. **Build the hull on the water** from wood (planks, logs, slabs, stairs, fences, doors...), wool and carpets (sails),
   and decorations (barrels, chests, banners, lanterns, ladders, chains, bells...). **No stone, metal or dirt.**
2. Put a **stair at the back** as the helm (the captain's chair) and **slabs** as seats for your crew.
3. Get an **engine**: buy one from the **Shipwright** in the market's Sailing stall, or craft it. Bigger engines
   carry bigger ships and are faster.
4. **Place the engine on the deck** (on blocks floating on water).
5. Look at your ship and type `/crimboat check`: it tells you if it can sail and what is wrong if not.

## Sailing

Sit on the helm stair: **W / S** sail forward and back, **A / D** steer, **Shift** leaves the helm. **Everyone on
board has to sit** (stairs or slabs) for the ship to move. When it stops it drops anchor and turns back into
normal blocks you can build on. Ships can't sail onto land or into unloaded water.

## Cannons and sinking

- **Ship cannons** go on the deck: right-click one to man it, aim with the mouse, click to fire. They need **ship
  cannonballs** (the Shipwright, or craft them: iron + gunpowder).
- Every ship has hull health: a small ship sinks after about 5 hits, the biggest after about 20 (bigger engines add
  a little). The health bar shows when it's hit. A sunk ship is gone, and any cargo on board goes overboard.
- Ship cannonballs also damage facility cores in a [siege](Sieges.md), and a Shore Battery fires back.

## Other ships at sea

- **Tidewatch sloops and pirate brigs** appear near players on the open sea. Board one, beat the crew and the
  captain, loot the chests and get off before it sinks. About one officer in five has a fruit power.
- **Merchant brigs** carry cargo crates for whoever takes the ship.
- Tidewatch forts have cannon batteries that fire at ships.

## Squalls

Out at sea in the **Abyssal Reach** (the far sea), a squall can catch you: rain, thunder and lightning striking a
few blocks away for about a minute, then a few minutes of calm. In a real thunderstorm it's a squall all the time.
Sail through it or turn back.
