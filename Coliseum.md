# The Coliseum

The Coliseum is where the big fights of Aquatic SMP happen: a sandstone arena island with two more islands next to
it, closed off from the rest of the sea.

## Getting there

Step into the **end portal at spawn**. You arrive on the plaza south of the arena, looking at it. The portal behind you
takes you back to where you came from. You can't use the portal while you are in combat.

Nobody gets onto the Coliseum islands any other way: a ring of invisible barriers surrounds them, and sailing,
swimming, flying or throwing ender pearls past it doesn't work. Events and tournaments bring their fighters there
themselves.

## The three islands

| Island | What's on it | What is fought there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Coliseum** | A sand pit with cover pillars and barred gates, six rows of seats, arcaded walls, four grand staircases and the plaza with the portal | Pirate FFA, the Pirate King tournament and the crew tournament |
| **The battle island** | A tall mountain with a shrine on the summit, smaller hills, forests, boulders and a beach | Treasure Island |
| **The Crew FFA island** | A big, mostly flat island: grass plains and forest, a sandy stretch, rocky ground, low hills, ponds and old ruins | Crew FFA |

The pit has an invisible roof and walls: nobody grapples or flies out of it into the stands, or in from the stands.
Spectators watch from the seats.

## Safe to fight

- **You never lose your items or XP** when you die on any of the Coliseum islands.
- Outside a running fight, **nobody can hurt anybody** there, not even while you wait for a fight to start.
- Nothing can be broken, built or blown up, nobody can claim the islands, and no mobs spawn.
- If the islands float in the sky, an invisible wall stands at the edge, and anyone who still falls off is put back.
