# Abyss Fruits

Rare fruits from treasure, crates and events. **Eat one for a power, only one per pirate.** There are 23 fruits; the full list with every move is on the [fruit list](Fruit-List.md), and in game in your encyclopedia (`/fruits`).

## Using your power
- **Use a move:** sneak + **F** (or sneak + right-click a block).
- **Pick a move:** sneak + **scroll** with an empty hand. Every move has its own cooldown.
- `/fruit` shows your power, your mastery and the cooldown.

## Rarity and class
| Rarity | Power |
| --- | --- |
| Common | ×0.9 |
| Uncommon | ×1.0 |
| Rare | ×1.15 |
| Epic | ×1.35 (+½ heart) |
| Legendary | ×1.6 (+1 heart) |

- **Body:** your body changes (Springcoil, Stoneskin, Ghostglass, Anchorweight, Titanseed, Lodestone, Rigline, Riftshift, Mirrorscale, Sirensong, Chronoshard).
- **Beast:** you become a beast (Behemoth, Venomfang, Galeborn, Dunemaw, Packfang, Krakenheart).
- **Elemental:** you become an element. Always Epic or Legendary (Emberheart, Rimefrost, Luminara, Thunderveil, Hollowvoid, Tidecaller).

## Mastery and awakening
Hitting players and mobs with your moves trains your power (moves that miss train nothing), up to **mastery 40**: more damage, more range, shorter cooldowns and extra hearts. At **mastery 25 your fruit awakens**: you unlock an ultimate fourth move.

## Combos
Fruit powers work together. A hit leaves its element on the target for a few seconds, and a hit of the right other element sets off a **combo** for extra damage. Team up with your crew:

| Combo | First | Then | Extra damage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shatter | ice (or freezing) | lightning | +35% |
| Firestorm | fire (or burning) | wind | +30% |
| Conduct | water (or standing in water or rain) | lightning | +30% |
| Icebreak | ice | earth or sand | +30% |
| Steam Burst | ice | fire (thaws them) | +25% |
| Eclipse | light | void | +25% |
| Scald | fire | water (puts the fire out) | +20% |
| Toxic Blaze | poison | fire | +20% |
| Sandstorm | earth or sand | wind | +20% |
| Overload | lightning | light | +20% |

Every fruit move has its own hit sound, and the biggest moves shake your screen, each fruit in its own way: Thunderveil jolts you, Stoneskin makes the ground rumble, Rimefrost frosts your screen, Hollowvoid drags you into darkness, Galeborn and Venomfang make your head spin.

## The sea's curse
In water you **sink**, get slow and weak, and **can't use your power**. Rain is fine. The one exception is **Tidecaller**, the water fruit: the sea accepts it, so you can swim and use it in water.

Watch out for **Abyssal Iron**: a hit with it shuts your power off for a few seconds.

## The fruit encyclopedia: `/fruits`
Every fruit you discover goes into your encyclopedia, one row per rarity, with every move. You discover a fruit by **picking it up, eating it, or being hit by its power**. Click a fruit for all its moves in chat. It shows how many pirates carry a power, never who.
