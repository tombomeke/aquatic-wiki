# Events

Staff (and the weekly schedule) start server events. When one starts, everyone sees it in chat and on a bar at the top
of the screen. `/seaevent info` shows what's running, `/seaevent join` joins it.

## Fights in the Coliseum

The three fight events are held on the [Coliseum](Coliseum.md) islands. Join with `/seaevent join` while the bar says
**Join now!**: you're taken to the island and wait there. When joining closes, everyone is frozen on a starting spot
for a short countdown, and then the fight begins.

- **Pirate FFA:** every pirate for themselves in the Coliseum pit. Last one standing wins.
- **Crew FFA:** crews fight on the big Crew FFA island. You need a crew; your crew starts together and you can't hurt
  your crewmates. The last crew with fighters left wins.
- **Treasure Island:** a chest waits in the shrine on top of the mountain. It stays **sealed for the first 5 minutes**
  (the bar counts down), so fight first. Once it opens, the first fighter to open it wins the chest's loot (diamonds,
  emeralds, golden apples, netherite scrap and more) and the prize money. Fall and you start again from a spot on the
  slopes.

**Nothing is lost when you fall:** no items drop and you keep your XP. Spectators watch from the Coliseum's seats and
can't hit or be hit. When the event is over, everyone goes back to spawn.

**Once you join, you're in:** until you're out, the event ends, or you give up with `/seaevent leave`, you can't use
other commands (no `/spawn`, `/home` or `/tpa`), you can't be teleported away and the Coliseum portal won't take you
home. Private messages (`/msg`, `/r`) still work. Leaving the arena or logging out puts you out of the fight.

When a fighter falls, everyone in the fight and in the stands hears a thud and a tolling bell.

## The tournaments

Both tournaments are fought in the Coliseum pit, one match at a time. Staff open them with `/seaevent` like any other
event; you sign up with `/seaevent join` (or the tournament's own `register` command).

### The Pirate King tournament

Every pirate for themselves. Anyone can sign up while registration is open (`/pkt register`). When registration
closes, the bracket is drawn at random: some players may get a bye into the next round. Every match is a **1v1** in
the pit, round after round, until two players are left for the **grand finale** (best of 3).

The winner becomes the **Pirate King**: a title, a crown, fireworks and prize money. **The crown is never lost**: it
stays with you even when you die. `/pkt bracket` shows the draw, `/hallofconquerors` every Pirate King so far.

### The crew tournament

Crew against crew. Crew officers sign their crew up (`/crewtournament register`) and pick three fighters
(`/crewtournament roster`). During the **qualifiers** crews earn points around the world: kills on other registered
crews, claiming and conquering islands, treasure and bounties (`/crewtournament standings`). The best crews go on to a
bracket of **team fights** in the pit, ending in a best-of-3 finale. The winning crew fills its crew bank, earns
reputation and goes into `/crewchampions`.

## Other events

| Event | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Treasure Hunt | A treasure is buried; hints narrow down where. First to dig it up wins |
| Pirate War | Two crews in an official timed war with a kill scoreboard |
| Bounty Hunt | Extra bounties on the most wanted pirates; the hunter who claims the most wins |
| Lost Island | An island rises from the sea, full of treasure, and sinks again at the end |
| King's Challenge | King of the hill at the spawn throne: the crew that holds it longest wins, the Pirate King's crew defends |
| Grand Raid | Waves of Tidewatch soldiers and a High Warden boss; rewards by damage dealt |
| Naval Battle | Kills at sea (in boats, in the water or on a ship's deck) score points for your crew. Allied crews score nothing off each other |

## Event prizes

Events can give Doubloons, crate keys and items, including the two special **Aquatic** weapons that can't be bought or
found anywhere else: see [Items](Items.md).
