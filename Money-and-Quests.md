# Money and quests

## Doubloons
The pirate currency. New pirates start with 2,000. You earn them from treasure, discovering islands, island income, bounties, quests and selling loot. `/doubloons` shows your balance, `/pay <player> <amount>` sends some, `/crew bank deposit <amount>` puts them in the crew treasury. Doubloons are whole numbers: `1.500`, `1,500` and `1.5k` are all fifteen hundred.

## Trade goods and cargo runs
The biggest money is in trade: your islands produce goods that you carry to a Harbor Exchange. How it works, being
laden, contracts and the Sea Lord: [Cargo and trade](Cargo-and-Trade.md).

## Harbor Market
Talk to the shopkeepers in the stalls at spawn. **Left-click** to buy, **right-click** to sell, **shift** for a stack or all. Every stall has one shopkeeper:

| Shopkeeper | Sells and buys |
| --- | --- |
| **Blacksmith** | The smithy: upgrades your weapons and armour |
| **Provisioner** | Food and drinks; buys your fish and farm produce, sells seeds and saplings |
| **Shipwright** | Boats, engines, ship cannons, maps and sailing gear |
| **Merchant** | Buys your loot (ores, rare finds) and monster parts; sells luxury goods (trims, the netherite upgrade template, discs, patterns) |
| **Carpenter** | Wood and decoration |
| **Quartermaster** | Pirate gear, powder and shot, and tools, blades and bows |
| **Crew Registrar** | Founding and joining crews |
| **Stonemason** | Stone, bricks, concrete and building supplies |

The **Harbourmaster** by the bounty board hands out delivery quests. Next to the paths you find the boards of the **Richest Pirates** (`/baltop` for the full list), the most wanted pirates and the server's **Supporters**.

Sell prices drop a little every time something is sold (for everyone) and recover over a few hours: the item shows today's price. You can earn up to 15,000 Doubloons a day by selling. The strongest gear (the Musket, the Hand Cannon, bombs) and the Seafoam Milkshake that removes your fruit are expensive on purpose.

## Quests
The villagers by the well of every village, and the **Harbourmaster** on the capital, have quests. Right-click one to open their quest menu: click a quest to accept it, click it again when it's done to hand it in, or shift + right-click to give it up. `/quests` opens your quest journal.

- **Deliveries:** you get a sealed parcel to bring to another village or the capital. Hand it to a quest villager there. The further, the better the pay.
- **Story quests:** some quests unlock the next one when you finish them.
- **Reputation:** each village remembers your help. Friend, Trusted, Honored and Hero of the Village pay more and unlock harder quests.

## Crates
Crate keys come from treasure and quests. Open a crate at its block; you can preview what's inside first.

## The tournaments
Two tournaments are fought in the [Coliseum](Coliseum.md):

- **The Pirate King tournament:** every pirate for themselves, 1v1 duels until one remains. The winner is crowned
  **Pirate King**, takes the throne in the capital and wears a crown that is never lost. `/pkt register`,
  `/pkt bracket`, `/hallofconquerors`.
- **The crew tournament:** crew against crew, with qualifiers and team fights. The winning crew fills its bank and earns
  reputation. `/crewtournament register`, `/crewchampions`.

All about both: [Events](Events.md).
