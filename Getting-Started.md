# Getting started

1. **Watch the intro.** On your first join a camera tours the capital: the Crew Registrar, the Shipbuilder on the pier, the Shipwright and the market. Then the guide book opens. Read it again any time with `/guide` (`/guide start`, `/guide ships`, `/guide sieges`, ...); the story of the sea is in `/lore`.
2. **You start with 2,000 Doubloons:** enough to **found a crew** (1,000, at the Crew Registrar or `/crew`) **and buy your first ship**, the Driftwood Raft (750, from the Shipbuilder at the end of the pier).
3. **Your starter kit:** a boat, food, tools and a **Tide Compass**. Hold it to see the way to an island: right-click points to a random island (every 2 minutes), sneak + right-click to the nearest one (every 5 minutes).
4. **You're safe for 30 minutes.** New pirates can't attack or be attacked for their first 30 minutes of playtime. `/pvp` shows how long you have left; `/pvp on` ends it early.
5. **Find islands.** `/island map` lists the nearest islands with direction and distance. Every island you discover for the first time pays Doubloons, rarer islands more.
6. **Join or found a crew** ([Crews](Crews.md)). Alone you can't claim islands. Then claim one: [Islands](Islands.md).
7. **Look for a red X** of wool on the ground: buried treasure. Dig straight down.

**Tips**
- Nothing can hurt you at spawn: no falls, mobs, fire or drowning, and no PvP.
- New pirates get a helpful tip in chat every few minutes. Turn them off with `/guide tips off`.
- The **end portal at spawn** takes you to the [Coliseum](Coliseum.md), where the fight events are held.
- The spawn town has the **Harbor Market** (shopkeepers), the **Blacksmith** (Smithy) and quest-givers.
- `/recipes` shows every custom crafting recipe.
- Don't log out during a fight: you'd die and drop everything (see [Combat](Combat-and-Gear.md)).
- Ate an Abyss Fruit? **You sink in water.** Keep a boat close.

**What next?** Once you have an island: pack its goods and sail them to an Exchange ([Cargo and trade](Cargo-and-Trade.md)),
build facilities ([Facilities](Facilities.md)), pick a siege window and learn to defend it ([Sieges](Sieges.md)).
Something unclear? [Questions and words](FAQ.md).
